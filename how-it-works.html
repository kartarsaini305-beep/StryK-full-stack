The Process

How StryK Works

From posting to payment — everything happens in hours, not days.

01

Register & Select Skills

Sign up in 2 minutes. Choose your skills — Design, Writing, Video, Dev, or more. Everyone starts at Level 1.

02

Browse Available Tasks

Your dashboard shows tasks matching your level and skills. No waiting — tasks are ready to book instantly.

03

Book & Enter Clarity Window

Book a task and get a 5–10 minute window to ask the client up to 2 questions. Then work begins.

04

Complete & Submit

Work within the agreed scope. Upload your deliverable directly through the platform before the deadline.

05

Get Paid Same Day

Client approves — payment released from escrow to your UPI the same day. Level up with every great rating.

01

Register & Choose a Plan

Free plan gives 3 tasks/month. Starter ₹99 gives 15. Pro ₹199 is unlimited. Pick what fits your volume.

02

Post Your Task

Fill in the title, description, deliverables, and deadline. Select freelancer level. System auto-calculates minimum price.

03

Pay into Escrow

Your payment is held securely in Razorpay escrow. Not released until you approve the work.

04

Freelancer Books & Delivers

A matched freelancer books and completes your task. You get notified when work is submitted.

05

Review & Approve

Approve to release payment. Request one revision if needed. Or raise a dispute for AI-assisted resolution.

The Clarity Window — StryK's Secret Weapon

Immediately after booking, a 5–10 minute window opens for the freelancer to ask the client up to 2 quick questions. This eliminates confusion, reduces revisions, and protects everyone's time. If the client doesn't respond — work begins with the original brief, and the client owns responsibility for the brief they wrote.
